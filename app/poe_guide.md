# Grip

Grip is a job-hunt toolkit for developers, job hunters and students: interview
prep, a STAR story bank, system design practice and a hiring pipeline tracker in
one place. The top navigation has six tabs: Prep, Stories, Arch Board, Quest,
Fly Me and Profile. The whole app is a free demo; signing in with GitHub keeps
progress.

## First-run setup (battle plan)

A short setup: your stack, your goal, your target role and, optionally, your
experience or a job posting. Grip then writes a battle plan: the interview
questions you are likely to face, saved as Stories with sample STAR answers to
rewrite, plus one custom Arch Board scenario for the role. Run it again from
Profile ("Set up your practice") or from the Next Up card ("Set it up").

## Prep (Interview Prep)

Adaptive flashcards and drills.
- About 50 technologies in 10 categories, each with prep notes and a quiz.
- Difficulty tiers from Clear Skies to Thunderstorm. Thunderstorm answers race
  a 20 second clock for 1.5x XP.
- After you answer, the question shows a short explanation of the right answer.
- Next Up picks your session: due reviews, your prep plan, or your weakest techs.
- Mock loop: a rapid-fire quiz, a system design and a behavioral prompt, scored
  as one interview.
- A readiness score for your plan or stack.
- XP and rank (Hatchling up to Nevermore) track momentum.
Go here when you want to practice technical questions or warm up for an interview.

## Stories (Story Bank)

A STAR story bank for behavioral interviews.
- Write Situation, Task, Action, Result stories for 8 competencies: Conflict,
  Failure, Leadership, Impact, Ambiguity, Influence, Mentoring, Delivery.
- Coverage shows which competencies have no story yet; Next Up points at the
  first gap.
- Drill prompts mode: a random interview question, then reveal your matching
  stories.
- Link a story to the system behind it and design that board on the Arch Board.
- Open a story and press Grade this story: AI rates each STAR section as
  Covered, Thin or Missing and gives the question an interviewer would ask next.
  Editing the story makes the grade stale until you grade again.
Go here when you want to prepare "tell me about a time" answers.

## Arch Board

System design practice canvas.
- 104 scenarios across Commerce, Fintech, Social, Realtime, Data and more, plus
  custom scenarios and search.
- Drag and wire 21 component types (API, database, cache, queue, CDN and more).
- Five steps: Add, Connect, Describe, Explain, Grade, with one next action at a
  time.
- Evaluate against the scenario checklist: what is missing, design warnings and
  interviewer follow-ups.
- A scale brief with the numbers to estimate.
- On an empty board you can start a 10-minute skeleton sprint: 2 minutes to
  scope, 8 to draw every box. The score stays hidden until you finish, and a
  sprint log tracks your times and the checks you miss most.
- AI grades your talk track section by section and quotes what earned credit.
- After that grade, a written defence round appears under the talk track: answer
  the follow-up questions and press Grade my answers. That grade is separate
  from board readiness.
- Full screen and share links for saved boards.
Go here when you want to practice system design interviews.

## Quest

Hiring pipeline tracker.
- Track recruiters and applications through Contacted, Applied, Interviewing,
  Offer and Rejected.
- Next Up and stage filters put due follow-ups first.
- Interview retros per contact: questions asked, what went well, what to improve.
- Funnel dashboard: reach, pace, conversion rates and time in each stage.
- Story matching shows which stories are ready for a contact's competency gaps.
- "Drill these in Prep" hands a contact's posting techs to Prep as a plan.
- "Import list" reads an existing list of applications from a .docx or .md file
  or pasted text.
Go here when you want to organize your job search.

## Fly Me

A tour of Grip with a guided walkthrough of every section. Go here for an
overview or to restart the tour.

## Profile

Settings, GitHub and rank.
- Link GitHub to detect techs from public repos; import a CV to add its techs to
  your prep stack.
- Rank and progress to the next tier.
- Target role, location and LinkedIn; fields save as you leave them.
- Show or hide Poe on Prep, reset scores, rerun setup, language and sign out.
